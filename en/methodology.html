Methodology

The adopted methodology was structured into a series of phases:

  1. First phase: Involved the exploration of the national ontology of points of interest using the SPARQL keywords required for the project drafting (OPTIONAL, DISTINCT, UNION, FILTER and REGEX, LIMIT, ORDER BY); in parallel, the datasets made available by the Municipality of Bologna were pre-processed using Python on the Google Colab platform.
  2. Second phase: The national ontology was extended with a local ontology dedicated to the urban points of interest of Bologna, developed using the Protégé platform.
  3. Third phase: Starting from the integrated CSV and the Turtle file of the local ontology, Gemini Pro and ChatGPT Plus were compared using three different prompting techniques, aiming to generate RDF triples consistent with the ontological model; subsequently, the performances of the different LLM models were compared and discussed.
  4. Fourth phase: Involved the integration of the generated RDF triples within the local ontology, the validation of the Knowledge Graph's consistency through automated reasoning, and the subsequent querying via SPARQL to verify the correct representation of the information.
  5. Final phase: The project website was created, published on GitHub, and designed to present the methodology, queries, prompts, and obtained results.
