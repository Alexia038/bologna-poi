Project Topic & Gap Analysis

Exploration of POI-AP_IT via SPARQL

The first phase involved the systematic exploration of the national Points of Interest ontology (POI-AP_IT), exposed with the IRI https://w3id.org/italia/onto/POI and queryable through the SPARQL endpoint https://schema.gov.it/sparql.

The objective was to understand the overall structure of the ontology, identify the main available classes and properties, and evaluate their capacity to represent urban points of interest relevant to the Bolognese use case. The exploration was initiated through generic queries on subject-predicate-object triples, filtering resources whose IRI belongs to the POI-AP_IT namespace, in order to obtain an overview of the entities actually modeled.

Subsequently, specific queries were formulated to list the classes (owl:Class) and properties (owl:ObjectProperty, owl:DatatypeProperty), retrieving their labels, comments, domains, and ranges, so as to identify concepts and relations potentially reusable for the project.

Finally, a targeted query was executed to verify the possible presence of concepts already relevant to the city of Bologna and urban services of interest (TPER bus stops, benches, public toilets, parking areas, porticoes, urban furniture), using filters based on REGEX applied to IRIs, labels, comments, and descriptions. The analysis of the results showed that POI-AP_IT offers a general semantic model for points of interest and some related properties, but does not explicitly cover several specific urban categories of the Bolognese context, thus revealing a gap to be filled.

Data Preprocessing and Normalization of Municipal Data

In parallel with the ontological exploration, a data preprocessing phase was launched using data from the Open Data portal of the Municipality of Bologna, with a specific focus on datasets related to urban furniture, public toilets, TPER bus stops, parking lots, and porticoes. The original datasets were heterogeneous both structurally (different column headers, formats, conventions) and lexically (non-uniform categories and descriptions), necessitating a preliminary normalization before proceeding with semantic integration.

The preprocessing operations were performed in a Python environment (Google Colab) and specifically involved cleaning missing or anomalous values, harmonizing column names, standardizing formats (for instance, for dates and coordinates), and aligning descriptive categories across the different datasets.

Google Colab – Preprocessing and dataset integration ↗

The result of this phase was a single CSV file with integrated and consistent data, ready to be mapped onto the identified ontological structures.

Semantic Gap Analysis

Based on the information derived from the exploration of POI-AP_IT and the analysis of the municipal datasets, a gap analysis was conducted to identify the concepts that the national model did not satisfactorily cover. In particular, it revealed the absence of specific classes and categories for representing urban services and infrastructure typical of the Bolognese context, such as TPER bus stops, detailed urban furniture (benches, bins, fountains), public toilets, parking areas, and, notably, the porticoes.

This analysis led to the definition of requirements for a local ontological extension that would be compatible with POI-AP_IT, but capable of modeling the categories present in the municipal datasets in a more articulated manner. The goal was to introduce new classes and properties only when strictly necessary, prioritizing the reuse of preexisting elements wherever they proved adequate for data representation.
